The Problem
Field intelligence dies in WhatsApp messages

Every day, your field team has conversations worth crores. Most of it never reaches the people who need it.

🔇

Intelligence Loss

Field reps capture valuable intel — competitor activity, upsell signals, risk flags — but it stays in their heads or scattered chats.

⏳

Delayed Visibility

Leadership learns about opportunities and risks days or weeks later — in weekly calls, if at all. By then, competitors have moved.

🚪

Knowledge Walks Out

When a field rep leaves, years of relationship context and market intelligence disappear overnight.

📊

CRM Doesn't Capture This

CRMs track deals and contacts. They don't capture "Developer X just bought land" or "Competitor Y is offering 2% lower rates."

Technology
Production Architecture & Tech Stack

Built for scale, security, and real-time intelligence distribution.

📱

Layer 1 — Capture

Field reps send notes via WhatsApp Business API (or web bot). Supports text, voice notes, and images. Multi-language: English, Hindi, Hinglish.

WhatsApp Business APITwilio / GupshupSpeech-to-TextVoice Notes
🧠

Layer 2 — AI Processing

NLP engine extracts entities, amounts, competitors, signals. Classifies into cross-sell / timing / risk. Validates against external data sources.

GPT-4 / Claude APIEntity RecognitionSignal ClassificationFuzzy MatchingRAG Pipeline
📡

Layer 3 — Distribution

Role-based dashboards with real-time push. Signals routed by type, region, and hierarchy. Cross-role notifications and feedback loops.

React / Next.jsWebSocketsRole-Based AccessPush NotificationsREST API

Not Just One Industry
Any industry with field teams has this problem

The demo shows construction finance, but the pattern is universal. Here's how FieldPulse adapts.

🏗️

Construction Finance / NBFCs

RMs → Zonal Heads → Business Head → Credit

Capture pre-RERA land intelligence, cross-sell LAP/LRD opportunities, monitor developer health, track competitor rates and TAT.

LAP Cross-sell Pre-RERA Intel Contact Gap Pipeline Urgency
💊

Pharma / Medical Devices

MRs → Area Managers → Regional Head → Compliance

Medical reps visit 8-12 doctors daily. Capture prescription trends, competitor product launches, formulary changes, KOL sentiment shifts.

New Prescriber Formulary Change Competitor Launch KOL Sentiment
🏪

FMCG / Retail Distribution

Sales Reps → ASMs → RSMs → National Head

Field reps cover 20-30 retail outlets daily. Capture shelf-share data, competitor pricing, stockout alerts, new store openings, promo effectiveness.

Stockout Alert Competitor Pricing New Outlet Promo Intel
🏦

Banking / Insurance

Agents → Branch Mgrs → Regional → Risk

Relationship managers meet HNIs and SMEs. Capture investment appetite shifts, competitor offers, churn signals, cross-sell triggers for wealth/insurance.

Wealth Cross-sell Churn Signal Renewal Window Market Shift
🏗️

Real Estate Brokerage

Agents → Team Leads → Brokerage Head

Agents tour properties and meet buyers daily. Capture pricing trends, buyer sentiment, inventory movement, developer incentive changes.

Price Drop Buyer Intent Stale Inventory Developer Incentive
🔧

Industrial / B2B Sales

Key Acct Mgrs → Sales Directors → VP Sales

Account managers visit factories and procurement teams. Capture RFQ signals, competitor displacement opportunities, contract renewal timelines, technical requirements.

RFQ Detected Renewal Window Competitor Threat Budget Cycle
